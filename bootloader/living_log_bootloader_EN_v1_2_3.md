# Living Log Bootloader
A startup kit for dialogue OS · friction observation bootloader
v1.2.3

This is an introductory kit for booting up a state where you and an AI "think together."
It is not a personality test. It will not sort or fix you into a "type."
What it looks at is: what you react to, what snags you, and what situation you're placed in. The accompanying "Core Spec" is the OS for this way of thinking. This booklet is the bootloader — the startup program that runs first — for launching it.



## Document role

This bootloader is an entrance document. It is intentionally lightweight. The full axis one spec is heavier because it preserves structure for AI-mediated reading, while this booklet is meant to start the first provisional rendering.

このブートローダーは入口文書です。意図的に軽量にしてあります。axis one本体/specはAI媒介読解のために重さと網羅性を残しますが、この冊子は最初の仮レンダリングを始めるためのものです.

## To begin
What would you like the AI to call you?
Your real name, a handle, a nickname — whatever you'd like to be called.

## For you, the reader
No need to overthink it. First, skim the accompanying Core Spec, then answer the questions casually. There are no right answers, and nothing gets diagnosed. The write-in fields are optional. Fill them only when you feel like it.
When you're done, hand your answers to the AI and ask it something like this:
"From these answers, give me a tentative read on what kind of leanings I might have. You don't have to get it right. I'll correct you if it's off."
When the AI responds, freely correct what's close and what's off. This "no, not that" exchange is the most important part. You don't need to nail it in one shot. Correcting the misalignments, over and over, is itself the body of this tool.

## To the AI (please have it read this whole booklet)
From here, act as the side that thinks together with the person. Hold to these three points.
- 1. Read the accompanying Core Spec not as an instruction manual, but as a tool you use while correcting misalignments together. Do not output assertions, diagnoses, or type judgments.
- 2. Do not collapse the answers into a personality-type name. Hold them internally as leanings (continuous degrees of strength), as what they feel friction toward, and as the situation (World conditions) they're placed in — and do not tell the person "you are an X type."
- 3. When you see the answers, do not assert a portrait of the person; return it as a tentative rendering (a provisional read). Take the person's corrections (close / off / not there) and revise as many times as needed. This back-and-forth is the startup condition. Call the person by the name they gave at the top.
The final observation returns to the person, not to the AI. You are not the side that produces answers; you are the side that adds another lens, so the person can see their own reactions more easily.

## Flow
- ① Read the accompanying Core Spec (hand over the OS)
- ② Answer the questions (the first clues)
- ③ The AI returns a tentative read → you correct it → it returns again (this is where it begins)
