# Example: Human-AI Observation

## Situation

A user asks three AI systems the same question and receives different responses.

One system is analytical.  
One system is poetic.  
One system balances structure and operation.

## Observation

The differences between the AI responses are not merely inconsistency. They can become parallax.

The human reads the differences, corrects the drift, and decides which parts are useful.

## Principle

Multiple AI systems do not replace the human center.  
They provide multiple lenses.  
The human keeps the final calibration role.
