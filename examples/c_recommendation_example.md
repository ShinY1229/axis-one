# Example: C-Recommendation

## Situation

A user repeatedly watches works such as *Tora-san*, *Midnight Diner*, or *The Solitary Gourmet*.

A normal recommendation system may continue recommending similar shows.

A C-Recommendation asks what kind of reaction structure is visible.

## Reaction structure

- everyday life rather than spectacle
- human warmth rather than elegance
- shops with presence rather than polished luxury
- repetition, quietness, and lived-in texture
- a feeling that the world continues outside the screen

## Possible cross-domain recommendation

Instead of only recommending another show, the system may suggest:

- a small local diner
- a neighborhood Chinese restaurant
- an old shopping street
- a modest inn
- a public bath or local food walk

## Principle

Do not read only the genre.  
Read why the person responded to it.
